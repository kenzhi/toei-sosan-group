## 2026 market snapshot

### Growth categories

1. **Health & wellness**: Sleep aids, mental health
2. **Pet products**: Premium food, smart devices
3. **Baby & kids**: Educational toys, safety gear
4. **DIY & tools**: Power tools, home maintenance
5. **Sustainable goods**: Reusables, recycled materials

### Crowded categories (red oceans)

- Phone cases (oversaturated, low margin)
- Generic supplements (FDA / regulatory risk)
- Low-price fashion (shipping eats margin)
- Generic electronics accessories (compete with China-domestic brands)

## Differentiation tactics

### 1. Packaging design
The "warehouse first look" drives click-through. Premium gifting feel, eco materials, Japanese labelling.

### 2. Bundles
Higher LTV than single SKUs. Example: device + consumables + accessories.

### 3. Brand story
Use "founder's why", "material obsession", and "manufacturing story" inside Amazon A+ Content.

### 4. Cross-border fulfillment
- Amazon FBA (NA / EU / JP)
- Rakuten fulfillment
- Yamato international cool delivery
- Overseas warehouses (FBA Multi-Channel)

## Pitfalls to avoid

- Relying on one product = one language page (account-suspension risk)
- Using unregistered brand names
- Children's products without safety certifications
- Chinese OEM factory address leaking into public info (cover with Amazon Brand Registry)
