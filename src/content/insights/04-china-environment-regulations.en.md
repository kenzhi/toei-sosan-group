## RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances)

- Scope: Electrical and electronic equipment
- China RoHS 2: Effective since 2016
- Restricted substances: lead, mercury, cadmium, hexavalent chromium, PBB, PBDE, 4 phthalates
- Mark: "China RoHS 2 compliant" mark required

## REACH (EU chemicals regulation)

- Applies to chemicals manufactured/imported in the EU at >1 tonne/year
- SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) list: currently 241 substances
- Information duty when SVHC concentration in article exceeds 0.1% (w/w)

## China-specific regulations

### 1. China RoHS 2
- Applies to E&E products on the China market
- Compliance mark required

### 2. Air Pollution Prevention Law
- VOC (volatile organic compounds) emission limits
- Painting and printing processes need exhaust treatment

### 3. Solid Waste Pollution Prevention Law
- Classification, handling, designated disposal sites
- Manifest system for hazardous waste

### 4. Environmental Protection Tax Law
- Tax on pollutant discharge
- Itemized for air, water, soil, solid waste, noise

## OEM factory due diligence checklist

1. **Environmental management certification**: ISO 14001 obtained?
2. **Pollutant discharge permit**: Legal emissions license
3. **RoHS / REACH test reports**: Product-level compliance
4. **Chemical management tables**: Across the supply chain
5. **Waste disposal contracts**: With licensed vendors
