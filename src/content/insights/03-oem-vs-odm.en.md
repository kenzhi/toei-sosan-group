## OEM vs ODM

| Aspect | OEM | ODM |
|------|-----|-----|
| Design | By client | By factory |
| Manufacturing | By factory | By factory |
| IP | Client | Shared / factory |
| Lead time | Short | Medium–long |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Flexibility | High | Lower (built on standard SKU) |

## Decision framework for your own brand

**Pick OEM when**:
- You want unique specs on an existing product category
- Full control over design and function
- Mass production is urgent
- Confidentiality is critical (e.g., patented tech)

**Pick ODM when**:
- You lack design resources (especially industrial design)
- A standard-category clone is enough
- Speed matters (launch within 6 months)
- You can't invest pre-launch validation time

## The realistic middle ground

Many successful brands run an **OEM + ODM hybrid**:
- Launch the base with ODM, fast
- OEM the parts where you need differentiation
- Gradually shift toward more OEM over time
