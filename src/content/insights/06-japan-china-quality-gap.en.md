## Why the gap exists

Chinese factories base on **GB (Guobiao)**, but the Japanese market demands three layers: **JIS + industry standards + each buyer's own specs**.

## 5 critical differences

### 1. Dimension tolerance naming and measurement

JIS: JIS B 0401 grades (f, g, h, etc.)
GB: GB/T 1800 grades (IT01–IT18)

**Example**: JIS h7 and GB IT7 are the same grade, but the actual commonly-used tolerances differ (metric vs imperial legacy).

### 2. Surface roughness

JIS: JIS B 0601 (Ra, Rz, Ry)
GB: GB/T 1031 (Ra), but **the calibration basis differs** (JIS follows ISO 4287, GB follows ISO 4287 + China-specific adjustments)

**Example**: Japan buyer asks for "Ra 0.8", Chinese factory reports "Ra 0.8 achieved" — actual measurement 1.6 μm. Calibration gap.

### 3. Coating / plating thickness

JIS: JIS H 8504 (plating), JIS K 5600 (paint)
GB: GB/T 4956 (plating), GB/T 13452 (paint)

**Example**: JIS H 8504 mandates XRF measurement, GB/T 4956 allows magnetic methods — same "20 μm" spec, different real numbers.

### 4. Food-contact safety

JIS: Food Sanitation Act + industry voluntary standards
GB: GB 4806.1–4806.11 (food safety national standards)

**Example**: Melamine tableware for Japan — GB 4806.7 compliant, but JIS-required formaldehyde extraction test must be added before import.

### 5. EMC

JIS: VCCI + Electrical Appliance and Material Safety Act
GB: CCC (China Compulsory Certification)

**Example**: Wi-Fi module for Japan — China CCC is enough, but Japan 技适 (Giteki) mark is still required to sell.

## Summary: 5 steps for Chinese factories to align with JIS

1. **Buy the official JIS standard** (from JSA, ¥2,000–¥5,000 each)
2. **Calibrate inspection equipment to JIS** (via Japanese calibration bodies)
3. **Raise sampling frequency** (GB AQL 4.0 vs JIS buyer AQL 1.0)
4. **Pre-agree the JIS-non-conformance handling flow**
5. **Run 100% inspection on the first batch** with the JIS buyer present
