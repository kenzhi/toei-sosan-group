## Why we're hiring

Toei Sosan Group has been growing fast on both axes—China OEM manufacturing and Japan-bound cross-border e-commerce—since our November 2025 founding. Two key roles to fuel the second half of 2026.

**What you'll do**:
- Tmall / Taobao / JD.com marketplace entry strategy
- China consumer marketing
- Negotiation with Chinese partners

**What we're looking for**:
- 3+ years China e-commerce experience
- Business-level Mandarin
- Existing Zhejiang / Guangdong supplier network

## Role 2: Factory Operations Manager (Dongguan)

**What you'll do**:
- Day-to-day operations at our Dongguan facility
- Production planning and quality control
- Manage a ~50-person line

**What we're looking for**:
- 5+ years manufacturing operations
- ISO 9001 internal auditor certified
- Native Mandarin, Cantonese a plus
