## Factory Overview

- **Location**: Chang'an Town, Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, China
- **Floor area**: 3,500 m²
- **Employees**: 80 (including 3 Japanese staff, 12 quality control)
- **Production capacity**: 500K units/month (general consumer goods)
- **Categories**: Precision assembly (electronics, daily goods), packaging, final inspection

## Features

### Self-Managed System

Directly operated by Toei Sosan Group, implementing Japanese quality management standards at the Chinese factory. Unlike third-party OEM factories, we ensure **process transparency** and **immediate response to improvements**.

### Japanese Staff On-Site

CEO Orita and Co-founder Han regularly visit the site for confirmation. Quality management leadership: 1 Japanese + 12 Chinese staff.

### One-Stop Service

- Material sourcing (Chinese domestic / Japanese import — both available)
- Prototyping (small batch from 100 units)
- Mass production (from 10,000 units)
- Inspection and logistics (through to FBA delivery)
