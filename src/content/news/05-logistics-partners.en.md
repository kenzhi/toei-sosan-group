## Why we expanded

Previously we shipped through a single partner warehouse with 5-7 day lead time. We rebuilt the logistics network to serve clients better.

## New partners

### Sagawa Express (international cool delivery)
- Next-day across Japan (excl. Okinawa / remote islands)
- International: China → Japan FBA warehouse in 3 days
- Up to 30 kg / parcel

### SF Express
- China domestic: same-day / next-day
- International: China → North America / Europe / SE Asia in 3-5 days
- Up to 70 kg / parcel

### DHL Express
- International express: 2-4 days
- DDP (duties pre-paid) supported
- Dangerous goods (lithium batteries, etc.) supported

## Service matrix

| Service | Lead time | Use case |
|------|------|------|
| Express international (DHL) | 2-4 days | High-value / urgent |
| Standard international (SF) | 3-5 days | B2B bulk |
| FBA inbound (Sagawa) | 3-5 days | Amazon restock |
| Last-mile (Sagawa) | Next day | Domestic e-commerce |

## Cost benefit

Mixing partners gets us an estimated **15-20%** annual logistics cost reduction, which we pass through to our clients.
