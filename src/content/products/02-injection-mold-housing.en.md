## Product Overview

Injection-molded housings for consumer electronics and electrical devices. Supports ABS / PC / PA66 and other engineering plastics.

## Manufacturing Flow

1. **Mold design**: 3D CAD (SolidWorks / CATIA) + CAE simulation
2. **Mold fabrication**: At partner mold shop (Shenzhen), CNC + EDM
3. **Molding**: 12 injection machines, 80T–800T capacity
4. **Secondary operations**: Printing, painting, assembly
5. **Inspection**: Dimensional + visual + functional testing

## Materials

- ABS (general-purpose, flame-retardant grades)
- PC (polycarbonate)
- PA66 (glass-fiber reinforced)
- POM (Delrin)
- PP (general-purpose)
- PMMA (acrylic)

## Application Examples

- Smart speaker enclosures
- Hair dryer / dryer housings
- Electric toothbrush bodies
- Industrial sensor enclosures
- Medical device exteriors (ISO 13485 partner facility)
