## Product Overview

Precision metal parts manufactured at Toei Sosan's Dongguan facility. ±0.02mm dimensional tolerance for automotive, medical, and industrial applications.

## Manufacturing Process

1. **Material sourcing**: A6061 aluminum blocks (domestic partner suppliers)
2. **5-axis CNC**: Mazak Integrex i-200 for one-shot complex geometries
3. **Dimensional inspection**: 100% inspection via CMM
4. **Surface treatment**: Anodizing (clear / color / hard) at partner facility
5. **Pre-shipment QC**: Sampling + 100% on critical features

## Typical Applications

- Automotive engine components (prototype + mass production)
- Medical device housings (FDA submission support)
- Semiconductor manufacturing equipment structural parts
- Industrial robot arm end-effectors

## Production Support

- Prototype: from 1 piece
- Small batch: from 100 pieces
- Mass production: from 1,000 / month
- Lead time: 2 weeks prototype / 45 days mass production
