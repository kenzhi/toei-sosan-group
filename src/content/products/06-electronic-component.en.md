## Service Overview

Custom electronic components for automotive electrical and industrial equipment. End-to-end from spec to prototype to certification to mass production.

## Component Types

- **Sensors**: temperature / pressure / proximity / optical
- **Connectors / harnesses**: waterproof (IP67/68), automotive-grade
- **Custom PCBs**: Small batches (10–1,000 units)
- **Assembled modules**: sensor + PCB + housing

## Certification Support

- **Automotive**: IATF 16949 partner facilities
- **Industrial**: CE / UL / RoHS
- **Medical**: ISO 13485 partner facilities
- **Explosion-proof**: ATEX / IECEx (optional)

## Development Flow

1. Spec consultation (with our engineers)
2. Drawings + 3D model
3. Prototype (in-house mockups)
4. Functional + reliability testing
5. Certification support
6. Mass-production transfer
7. Continuous improvement post-launch
