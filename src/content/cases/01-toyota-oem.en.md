## The Challenge

This Japanese auto parts brand had produced domestically for years, but rising labor costs and shorter lead-time requirements pushed them to consider overseas production. However, after outsourcing to Chinese factories, they couldn't meet the quality standard (especially dimensional tolerance of ±0.02mm), and failed with three factories in a row.

## Our Approach

Toei Sosan first stationed a Japanese quality management team at the Dongguan factory for two weeks, redesigning the entire manufacturing process (material receiving → machining → inspection → shipping). We created three prototype batches, measuring dimensions, surface roughness, and hardness at every stage.

## Results

- Completed mass-production samples in **12 weeks**
- Achieved dimensional tolerance of **±0.015mm** (higher precision than the ±0.02mm required)
- **35% reduction** in mass-production costs
- First-year delivery defect rate of **0.3%** (equal to domestic factories)
